You have the blanket of being new so ask more questions than you make mistakes
You can always say "Oh, I'm new here can you explain the reasoning behind x?" and get away with it every time. They will be more patient towards you and if it's a decent team then welcoming too!

Execute parallely as you ideate. Find out problems. Don't wait for tasks to be given start by learning the system and share updates to your lead.
